Privacy & backups.

Entries are stored only in this browser's local storage. No account or cloud sync is involved.

Keep control of your journal

Browser storage is not encrypted. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to read it. Clearing browsing data or switching devices may erase it.

If you used the previous version, its saved dates and mood labels can be carried forward without rewriting their original date text.

◌A journal, not a diagnosis

This tool doesn't assess burnout, predict wellbeing or provide therapy. If your feelings become hard to manage, consider speaking with someone you trust or a qualified professional. If there's immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
